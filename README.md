## About

I'm a developer from Haryana, India, focused on building web and mobile products that are clean to look at and simple to use. I care about the details that make an app feel finished: responsive layouts, clear navigation, sensible structure, and real backend integration instead of placeholder data.

Most of my work is in React, Next.js, and Expo (React Native), with Supabase or Firebase handling auth, data, and storage. I ship my projects publicly, contribute to open source when I find something I can improve, and I'm always trying to get better at both design and engineering.
